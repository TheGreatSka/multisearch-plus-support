Privacy policy for Safari on Mac

Effective October 9, 2026. This policy covers MultiSearch Plus for Safari, the free Mac app and Safari extension.

Searches and preferences

MultiSearch Plus for Safari stores search history, saved searches, presets, and preferences locally on your Mac. These settings are not uploaded to a developer account. Use the extension’s controls to remove saved and recent searches.

Game-title suggestions

Suggestions use a bundled catalog and online game-catalog requests to app.lizardbyte.dev (LizardByte GameDB). Online requests include a bucket selected from the first characters of your search, and may include platform or game identifiers. The provider receives your IP address and normal network-request information. Do not enter sensitive information in a search.

Store websites

When you search, the extension opens tabs in Safari. Your query and relevant filters are sent to the stores you select through their search-page addresses. Those websites may receive your IP address and use cookies and website storage. Accounts, payments, shipping, and checkout are handled by the stores under their own privacy policies. The extension does not require a separate account.

Optional feedback

Only when you submit feedback, the extension sends the feedback type, your message, and the app version to our Google Apps Script service. Feedback may be stored in a Google sheet and delivered to the developer by email. Please do not include sensitive information. We keep submitted feedback while it is being addressed, then delete it from our feedback sheet and notification emails within 90 days after it is resolved. Contact us with enough information to identify your submission if you wish to request deletion.

Advertising and tracking

The Safari extension does not include an advertising SDK, sell personal information, or use data for tracking across other companies’ apps and websites. Store websites and catalog or feedback service providers operate under their own policies. The practices of the separate iOS app are described in its own privacy policy.

Questions and changes

Email appmultisearch@gmail.com with privacy questions or feedback-deletion requests. Requests concerning store websites or service-provider records may need to be directed to those providers. This policy will be updated if the extension’s practices change.
