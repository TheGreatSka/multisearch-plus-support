How can we help?

Questions, problems, or ideas for MultiSearch Plus? Use Options → Send Feedback in the app, or email us.

Contact support

Start a search

Type a game title, optionally choose a system, select one or more stores, and tap Search. Select a store tab to browse its results. Results load from each store’s own website.

Full Access and restoring purchases

Full Access is a one-time purchase that removes the app’s ads and unlocks two-store comparison on supported larger displays.

To restore a previous purchase, open Options → Restore Purchases using the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Retailer websites may still display their own ads.

A store page won’t load

Try refreshing the page and check your internet connection. A store may occasionally be unavailable or require an action on its website.

If you send feedback, include the store name and search title. Please leave out passwords and payment details.

Saved searches

The app includes controls to remove individual searches and clear saved or recent searches. Local data can be lost when the app is deleted.
